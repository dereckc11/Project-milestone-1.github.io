Examples of Major Browser Projects

Different browsers share many basic functions, but their engines, platform integration, privacy settings, and ecosystems can differ.

Google Chrome

Chrome is based on Chromium and Blink, and offers broad website compatibility and a large extension ecosystem.

Mozilla Firefox

Firefox uses Gecko and provides customization and privacy controls, including protections against known trackers.

Apple Safari

Safari uses WebKit and is closely integrated with Apple's operating systems and device features.

Microsoft Edge

Modern Edge is based on Chromium and includes Microsoft platform and service integrations.

What's the best one?

No browser is best for every person. Consider standards compatibility, updates, accessibility, privacy controls, extension permissions, battery use, and the devices you use and how compatible the browser is to your device.
