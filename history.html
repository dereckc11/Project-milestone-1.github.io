A Short History of Web Browsers

The browser evolved alongside the World Wide Web and the growing expectations of internet users.

1991–1993: The early web

Tim Berners Lee's WorldWideWeb software appeared in 1991. CERN released the web software into the public domain in 1993, and NCSA Mosaic helped make browsing accessible to more people.

1994–2000: Browser competition

Netscape Navigator and Microsoft Internet Explorer competed intensely. Rapid feature development helped popularize the web, but inconsistent browser behavior made standards especially important.

2000s: More choices

Firefox, Safari, and other browsers expanded competition. Web standards and richer internet connections supported more complex layouts, multimedia, and web services.

2008 onward: Modern platforms

Chrome's launch helped popularize a minimalist interface and multiprocess architecture. Today's browsers support online documents, video meetings, shopping, learning, and many other application like experiences.
